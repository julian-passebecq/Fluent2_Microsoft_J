## What is logically sound in the current fixtures

JOIN compiles one result per matching pair, with pair IDs encoding both source record IDs. NULL join keys do not match. Unique-key results are four LEFT rows or three INNER rows. Adding `C1-copy` preserves record identity separately from the repeated key and creates all four C1 pairs; totals become six/five. Mode and dataset comparisons use seven aligned frames. Current tests independently enumerate these pair sets.

The bubble sort fixture preserves item IDs, records adjacent comparisons and swaps, and shortens the unsorted region. It ends at 1–5. Existing tests verify permutation, monotonic completed suffix length and final order; they do not independently verify every individual comparison/caption. The inspected fixture logic is consistent, but future algorithm changes need their own operation-level invariants.

The authored retry trace keeps Source and Transform successful, holds Publish blocked while Quality retries, and releases Publish only when Quality succeeds. That behavior comes from the authored trace, not dependency enforcement by the compiler. Manual player seeking pauses; timers cancel on effect cleanup; current equal-length variants avoid invalid indexes.
